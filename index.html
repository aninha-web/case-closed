A personal tracker for finished crime, detective and investigation series in English, first aired in 1990 or later.

Series, posters and cast come from TMDB, a free film and TV database. The app needs your own free TMDB key to load them.

  1. Create a free account at themoviedb.org.
  2. Open Settings, then API, and request a key for personal use.
  3. Copy the API Key and paste it here.

The key is stored only on this device. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
